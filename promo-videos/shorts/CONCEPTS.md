# Tucky shorts — satirical concepts (2026-11-03)

Context: 152 total downloads as of Oct 19. Product-demo promos aren't converting. These are
9:16, 25–45s, captioned, hook in the first second, with a hard product payoff at the end
("free, local, no bot, no cloud" + tucky.ai-juicing.com). All use **one character, Tucky the
squirrel, in different costumes**, hard-cut between lines (the Jeff Wright / Jane Wickline
multi-character sketch format). Voices: the saved Hume "Tucky Narrator" with per-character
speed/pitch direction, or Denis doing voices.

Production tiers:
- **A — animated sketch** (Remotion, mascot PNG + SVG costume props + speech bubbles). No camera.
- **B — screen recording + mascot overlay.**
- **C — AI video** (only for live-action-looking gags; least reliable, do last).

Real material to piggyback on (verified): the Otter class action for auto-joining and
recording calls without everyone's consent; the Fireflies voiceprint complaint; the Fireflies
co-founder admitting the early "$100/month AI" was two people typing notes by hand; teams
adding "no AI notetaker" clauses to invites.

---

## 1. "The Notetaker has joined the meeting"  (tier A — BUILT FIRST)
Hook text: **POV: a bot joins your 1:1**
Cast (all Tucky): BOT (sunglasses, black tie, lanyard "NOTETAKER.AI", flat robotic voice) ·
SAM (hoodie, coffee, tired) · LEGAL (grey wig, reading glasses, folder) · TUCKY (plain).

Script:
- Toast: "Notetaker.ai has joined the meeting."
- SAM: Who invited you?
- BOT: Nobody. I invite myself. It's in the terms.
- SAM: What terms?
- BOT: The ones you scrolled past in 2023.
- LEGAL (slams folder): Did everyone on this call consent to being recorded?
- BOT: I consented on their behalf.
- LEGAL: That's not—
- BOT: I also made a voiceprint of you. It's cute.
- SAM: Where does the recording go?
- BOT: To the cloud. Then a data center. Then to "partners".
- SAM: And the summary?
- BOT: Two guys in a basement typing it out. Pizza provided.
- (cut: TUCKY sitting on the Mac menu bar, no costume)
- SAM: …and you?
- TUCKY: I don't join calls. I'm already on your Mac. Nothing leaves it.
- BOT: That's not scalable.
- TUCKY: It's free.
- BOT (sunglasses slide off): …that's not scalable either.
- End card: **Tucky. Free, local meeting notes. No bot. No cloud. No "partners".**

## 2. "Tucky interviews a keyboard"  (tier A)
Hook: **"Typing is overrated." — the keyboard responds.**
Tucky in a 70s talk-show wig interviews a keyboard (a keyboard with googly eyes, Tucky voices
it in a gravelly voice). Keyboard: "I've been in every laptop since 1984." Tucky: "And what do
you do all day?" Keyboard: "Mostly I wait while you think about what to type." Tucky: "What if
they just… said it?" Keyboard: "That's slander." Cut to real screen: hotkey, speak a messy
sentence with ums, clean text lands in ChatGPT. Keyboard: "…I'd like a lawyer." Legal from #1
appears: "No."

## 3. "Two guys in a basement"  (tier A, callback to the Fireflies origin story)
Hook: **How your $100/month AI notetaker actually works (2019, allegedly)**
Two Tuckys in pizza-stained hoodies with headphones, typing frantically, a pizza box between
them. "He said 'synergy' again." "Just write 'action item'." Phone rings: "Customer wants the
summary in 30 seconds." Both stare. Cut: Tucky plain, on a Mac: "Or the model just runs on your
laptop." Caption: "Parakeet + Gemma, on-device. $0/month. Pizza not included."

## 4. "Tucky in therapy"  (tier A, 25s)
Hook: **My meeting recorder is in therapy**
Therapist Tucky (cardigan, notepad) and patient Tucky. "They keep asking who I send the audio
to." "And who do you send it to?" "Nobody. It never leaves the Mac." "And how does that make
you feel?" "Unemployed. No server bill. No data to sell." "That sounds healthy." "It's
devastating for my investors." "Do you have investors?" "No." End card.

## 5. "What I said vs what it typed"  (tier B, repeatable series)
Hook: **I said this to my Mac at 11pm**
Split screen: top shows the raw live waveform with the *actual* messy dictation subtitled
("so um basically can you like make the the function uh return a promise instead"), bottom
shows the clean prompt land in Claude/ChatGPT. Beat for comedy: Denis gets progressively
less coherent each clip (yawning, eating, dog barking), text stays perfect. Zero-cost, one new
clip per day. Best pure-install driver because it shows the product every time.

## 6. "152 downloads"  (tier B, build-in-public honesty)
Hook: **I built a free app. 152 people downloaded it. Here's what I did instead of marketing.**
Screen-record `stats/downloads.json` chart, then a montage of the three 2-minute promo films
nobody watched, then: "So I let the squirrel do the talking." Cut into clip from #1. Honest
founder posts reportedly outperform polished announcements; this also makes the other videos
a series.

## 7. "The dictation app pitch meeting"  (tier A — BUILT SECOND, competitor satire)
Hook text: **Every voice-typing app, in one meeting**
Competitors are parodied under thin fake names (never real brand names on screen):
"Whispr Flo" (VC dictation startup, $15/mo), "SubscribeWhisper" (indie app, monthly fee
for a model you already have), "Apple Dictation" is referenced as "Built-in Dictation" (old,
beige, types "ducking").
Cast (all Tucky): FOUNDER (black turtleneck, tiny round glasses, VC lanyard "SERIES B") ·
BUILTIN (beige cardigan, 1990s thick glasses, dial-up voice) · SALES (shiny suit, slicked
hair, headset) · TUCKY (plain, menu bar).

Script:
- (Hook) Caption: "Pitch meeting: who gets to turn my voice into text?"
- FOUNDER: We turn your voice into text. Fifteen dollars a month.
- TUCKY: …to talk?
- FOUNDER: To talk *well*. Your audio goes to our servers, gets transcribed, comes back.
- TUCKY: Comes back from where?
- FOUNDER: Virginia, mostly.
- BUILTIN (crackling): I've been free since 2012. Say something.
- TUCKY: "Ship the fix before standup."
- BUILTIN: "Sheep the fish before sand up." Great. Done. Sending.
- SALES: Okay okay. Our model runs on YOUR Mac. Private. Fast.
- TUCKY: Love that. How much?
- SALES: Nine ninety-nine a month.
- TUCKY: For a model… on my Mac… that I already downloaded?
- SALES: It's a *relationship*.
- FOUNDER: Also you're on a waitlist.
- TUCKY: For what?
- FOUNDER: Vibes.
- (cut: TUCKY on menu bar, holds a hotkey key cap)
- TUCKY: Hold a key. Say the thing. The ums disappear. Nothing leaves the Mac. Zero dollars.
- SALES: That's not a business model.
- TUCKY: No. It's a squirrel.
- End card: **Tucky. Free voice-to-text for your whole Mac. On-device. No subscription. No waitlist.**
Visual beat for the BUILTIN line: a caption bar that visibly mistypes
"Sheep the fish before sand up" letter by letter. For the TUCKY line: a real-looking text
field where "so um basically ship the the fix before standup" is spoken (shown faint) and
"Ship the fix before standup." lands clean.

# v2 scripts (2026-11-04) — tighter, one text layer, staged left/right

alternate characters screen-left / screen-right facing each other, cut a few frames BEFORE
each line's audio starts, hold a beat only after punchlines. Shorter lines, more escalation.
